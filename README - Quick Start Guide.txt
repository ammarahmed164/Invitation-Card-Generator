========================================================================
  INVITATION CARD GENERATOR — Quick Start Guide
========================================================================

Create beautiful invitation cards in your browser.
No install needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) OPEN THE APP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Double-click  index.html
  OR
• Double-click  Launch Builder (Windows).bat
  OR (Mac)
• Double-click  Launch Builder (Mac).command

Tip: Chrome or Edge works best.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) PICK A TEMPLATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Choose a collection (Wedding, Engagement, Baby Shower, etc.)
2. Click a suite/template
3. Studio opens — start customizing

Top bar → Collections = go back to all templates anytime.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3) EDIT YOUR CARD (easy steps)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
FRONT / BACK
• Use Front / Back buttons on the top bar to switch sides.

TEXT
• Click any text on the card to select it
• Edit in the floating dock (or left Text tab)
• Change font, size, color, alignment
• Drag text to move it
• Ctrl/Cmd + click = select multiple texts
• Arrow keys = nudge selected items

PHOTOS / IMAGES
• Left sidebar → Photo
• Upload your photo or replace an existing one
• Click the photo on the card → resize / reposition
• Use the live dock to swap the image quickly

QR CODE (RSVP / links)
• Left sidebar → QR RSVP
• Paste your link (RSVP, maps, website)
• Adjust size and side (front/back)
• One QR per side — edit anytime

PAPER / LOOK
• Left sidebar → Paper for textures & colors
• Elements tab for seals, borders, accents

BLEED / ZOOM
• Bleed = print trim guides (turn off for a clean preview)
• Zoom + / − to inspect details

GUEST VIEW
• Top bar → Guest View
• See how guests will experience the finished card

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4) EXPORT & SHARE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Top right you will see a green WhatsApp button — use this first.

• WhatsApp (green button)
  - One-tap share of your invite card
  - Mobile: choose WhatsApp from the share sheet
  - Desktop: image downloads + WhatsApp opens
    → attach the downloaded image in the chat

Or open Export for more options:

• Share to WhatsApp (featured in the menu)
• Print-Ready PDF (300 DPI) — best for print shops
• Digital PNG — Instagram / stories
• Email JPG — lighter email file

Wait for the short “Exporting…” screen — then check Downloads if needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5) PRINT TIP (short)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Size: 5" × 7"
• Paper: thick cardstock (~300 GSM)
• Send the Print-Ready PDF to your printer

------------------------------------------------------------------------
That’s it — pick a suite, edit text & photos, export, done.
